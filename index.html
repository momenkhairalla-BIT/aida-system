02 · Competitor-Inspired UX

Benchmarked against the best café apps

We study what leading Malaysian and regional café loyalty apps do well — then design something original for Aida. No logos, no copying: only the lessons worth keeping.

Z
ZUS Coffee
App-first loyalty

Rewards and points are the hero of the home screen, with frictionless app-based ordering and constant reward visibility.

Make Aida Points impossible to miss on the home screen.
C
Coffee Bean · MyCBTL
Order-ahead & member perks

Strong order-ahead flow with clear member benefits and tiered rewards that make membership feel valuable.

Give scheduled order-ahead a premium, effortless flow.
S
Starbucks
Polished rewards & mobile order

Best-in-class polish: refined star rewards, mobile order & pay, and a highly consistent visual system.

Hold every screen to a flagship level of polish.
T
Tealive
Promos & birthday rewards

Playful, frequent promotions — monthly deals, birthday rewards, and campaigns that drive repeat visits.

Build a lively promo & birthday reward engine.
G
Gigi Coffee
Wallet · scan · pay · pickup

Smooth stored-value wallet with scan-to-pay and quick pickup — payments feel instant and modern.

Wallet + QR scan-to-pay as a first-class flow.
B
Bask Bear Coffee
Strong brand personality

A distinctive, memorable brand character that carries warmth and identity across the whole experience.

Let Aida's warm café personality lead the UI.
K
Kenangan Coffee
Pickup · vouchers · campaigns

Efficient pickup, generous voucher stacking, and loyalty campaigns that keep customers returning.

Voucher & campaign tooling built into admin.
GF
GrabFood / Foodpanda
Secondary ordering UX

Reference only for ordering patterns: clear menus, modifiers, cart clarity, and order tracking.

Borrow menu & cart clarity — keep loyalty ours.

B · Staff POS Tablet

Fast, touch-friendly, mistake-resistant

The POS keeps baristas moving. Scan a member, tap products from a clear grid, apply offers in one touch, and confirm a checkout that always shows the final total.

  • Scan member QR to attach points instantly
  • Big touch targets — fast, mistake-resistant taps
  • One tap to apply verified student discount
  • Clear cart totals before every checkout

Professional, clear, owner-ready

  • At-a-glance KPIs for sales, members, and rewards
  • Sales trends and top products in one view
  • Live transaction feed with reward tracking
  • Manage menu, offers, and promotions centrally

04 · Design Principles

The rules that keep Aida premium

One consistent visual system across three interfaces — each optimised for the people who use it, and all held to a commercial standard.

01
Customer app: beautiful & rewarding

Simple, warm, and reward-focused. Every screen makes points, stamps, and offers feel valuable and effortless.

02
Staff POS: fast & mistake-resistant

Touch-friendly targets, clear flows, and confirmation before checkout keep baristas quick and confident.

03
Admin dashboard: clear & owner-ready

Professional, legible reporting that gives the owner instant confidence in sales, members, and rewards.

04
Commercially competitive

A real, market-ready café product that stands beside the best Malaysian café apps — original and premium.
